Rebuilt, not traced: the crest group (g3132) of [[:File:Emblem of Nobuhito, Prince Takamatsu (Collar of

recoloured black and dark-gold outlines white, scaled ×3.004 and centred on the PNG's ink (`build.py`).

the petal separations a little wider than the PNG does; thinning its strokes did not change the overlap, so
the difference is in the drawing, and the source is kept as drawn. No text. Licence: CC BY-SA 3.0.
