Built by `files/yamanashi-gun/build.py yamanashi` from MLIT 国土数値情報 N03 boundaries:
- Yamanashi's current (2024) municipalities in white with black borders, and the neighbouring prefectures in grey, in an
  equirectangular projection fitted to the PNG's Yamanashi outline (IoU 0.99);
- the Meiji-era 山梨郡 in yellow: the union of its municipalities in the N03 data of 1920 (東・西山梨郡; the districts of 1878
  still stood then), trimmed to the current prefecture outline, with the PNG's grey line where the old district edge
  cuts a current municipality;
- prefecture borders in red, the Fuji Five Lakes (W09) and Sagami Bay in cyan with a blue rim;
- the legend constructed, with 現在 / 明治期 / 郡役所 as real `<text>` fitted to the PNG's lettering (MS Gothic, with
  IPAGothic / Noto Sans CJK JP as fallbacks).
