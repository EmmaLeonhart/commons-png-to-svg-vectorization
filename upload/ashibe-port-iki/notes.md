# File:Position of Ashibe Port Iki City.png → SVG

Built by `files/ashibe-port-iki/build.py` (the Iki series build) from MLIT 国土数値情報 N03 (2024) boundaries of Iki City:
every island in an equirectangular projection fitted to the PNG's land mask, the PNG's colours and black coast, and
the port's shore in blue. That shore is the real coastline inside the PNG's blue-marked area, mapped back through the
fitted projection; the head of the mark falls on OpenStreetMap's 芦辺港 (bay, 33.8078 N, 129.7560 E). The labels
壱岐島 and 芦辺 / 漁港 are real `<text>` fitted to the PNG's lettering (MS Gothic, with IPAGothic / Noto Sans CJK JP as
fallbacks).

As for the other Iki maps, the PNG's outline is a schematic hand drawing, so the real outline replaces it (land IoU
0.76). Comparison: `Documents\claude-screenshots\agentic-vectorization_2026-10-20\cmp_ashibe-port-iki.png`.
