# File:Amateur radio compact icon.png → SVG

Built by construction (`files/amateur-radio-icon/build.py`): the black-bordered yellow square, the antenna
(triangle, centre line, mast) and the five ground bars measured from the PNG, and the coil as a regular
helix seen side-on (a trochoid) with its start, pitch (22.5 px, measured) and phase fixed and its width,
loop height, length and stroke fitted. Ink IoU 0.89.

The call (2026-10-19, the open coil question): the PNG's coil is a hand-drawn spline whose loops are
lopsided (wide flat fronts, tall backs). Fitting a chain of curves to those strokes would be tracing, so the
regular coil stands in for it; it spans the same height with the same number of turns, but its loops are
rounder than the PNG's. Comparison:
`Documents\claude-screenshots\agentic-vectorization_2026-10-19\cmp_amateur-radio-icon.png`.
