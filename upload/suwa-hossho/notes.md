The red banner with the inscription 南無諏方南宮法性上下大明神 as real `<text>` in gold (`files/suwa-hossho/build.py`).
- It is set vertically as one `<text>` with a positioned `<tspan>` per character.
- The characters are spaced evenly down the PNG's column of lettering and sized to its width.
- The face is kaisho (brush), outlined in the same gold (10 px) so its weight matches the brush lettering: the amount

letters done earlier. The text is the same.
