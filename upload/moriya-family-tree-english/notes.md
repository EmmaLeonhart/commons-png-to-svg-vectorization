# Moriya Family Tree (English)

- **Target:** [File:Moriya Family Tree (English).png](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Moriya_Family_Tree_(English).png),
  3872×1840, by Pat457 (2021), own work, after the 神長守矢氏系譜.
- **Rebuilt from scratch.** The chart is lines plus text, so:
  - 19 lines (8 horizontal, including three "=" pairs, and 11 vertical), 5 px thick as in the PNG, drawn
    exactly on the measured bands;
  - 28 text lines as `<text>` (black and `#6090dc`, as in the PNG): Latin names in a serif
    (`Gentium Plus` first, falling back to Noto Serif / DejaVu Serif) and the Japanese names in
    `Noto Serif CJK JP`. Sizes and positions were fitted per line to the PNG's ink boxes (within a few
    px at this size; see `params.json`, produced by `scratch/fit_text_lines.py`). Letter-spacing is
    kept only for the Japanese lines, because Latin spacing fitted to the local fallback font would
    not carry over to Commons.
  - The Japanese text was checked against a zoomed sheet of the PNG.
- **Note:** the original's Latin font is a Gentium-like serif. On Commons the Latin glyphs will
  follow the server's font, so widths may differ slightly from the PNG (each line stays centred).
