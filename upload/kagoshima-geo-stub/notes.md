# File:Kagoshima-geo-stub.png → SVG

Flag map for stub templates (221×338), made from the raster Shadow picture of Kagoshima prefecture.png and
Flag of Kagoshima.svg. Rebuilt with `tools/rebuild_flagmap.py`:
- **Outline**: MLIT 国土数値情報 N03 (2024) administrative areas, Kagoshima merged (`outline.py`), limited to
  what the PNG shows (north of 30.0 N: mainland, Koshikijima, Tanegashima, Yakushima and the small islands;
  not Tokara or Amami). Plain lon/lat as the PNG (fitted y/x 1.11). Silhouette IoU 0.853: the PNG's older
  outline differs around Kagoshima Bay and Sakurajima.
- **Flag**: [[:File:Flag of Kagoshima Prefecture.svg]] (PD), fitted inside the silhouette; 97.7% of flag
  pixels agree. Black outline as the PNG.
