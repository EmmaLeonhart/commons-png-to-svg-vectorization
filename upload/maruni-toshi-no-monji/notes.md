# File:Maruni-Toshi no Monji.jpg → SVG

Kamon *maru ni toshi no moji* (the character 利 in a ring). Built by construction: the ring measured from the
JPG's radial ink profile (centre 130.5, 131; inner radius 104.5, outer 130.5), and 利 as real `<text>` in
Noto Serif CJK JP at weight 900 (Black), sized and placed to the character's ink box (200 px, scaled 0.979
horizontally), so svgtranslate and font substitution keep working.

Difference from the JPG: its 利 is a heavy brush-style face; Noto Serif CJK JP Black is the heaviest CJK face
Commons renders, so the strokes are thinner and the serifs typeset (12% of pixels differ, IoU 0.80). Decided
2026-10-19 under the vector-text rule rather than tracing the brush outlines. Local render font:
`data_lake/downloads/fonts/noto-cjk/NotoSerifCJKjp-Black-subset.otf` (the one glyph, from notofonts/noto-cjk,
OFL). Comparison: `Documents\claude-screenshots\agentic-vectorization_2026-10-19\cmp_maruni-toshi.png`.
