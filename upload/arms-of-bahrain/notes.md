# Arms of Bahrain

- **Target:** [File:Arms of Bahrain.png](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Arms_of_Bahrain.png), 1107×1238,
  "Coat of arms of Bahrain minus the mantling", derivative by Salavat (2011) of Emblem_of_Bahrain.svg. PD-self.
- **Rebuilt from:** the 2008-02-04 revision of [File:Emblem of Bahrain.svg](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Emblem_of_Bahrain.svg)
  by Tonyjeff (PD-self / CC0), the revision current in 2011. Its shield path (the top-level red path) is
  scaled to the PNG's extent. That revision's white chief only covers the left of the shield (the rest is
  under the mantling), so the full-width five-point chief of the PNG is built from measured tips (red tips
  at y 111, white tips at y 282.5, every 222.75 px) and clipped to the shield. 3 px black outline.
- **Method:** `python files/arms-of-bahrain/build.py`. 0.62% of pixels differ from the PNG.
