# File:Map-okinawa-pref.png → SVG

Built by `files/map-okinawa-pref/build.py` from MLIT 国土数値情報 N03 (2024) boundaries of Okinawa Prefecture (the Daitō
Islands left out, as on the map): the islands in an equirectangular projection fitted to six island positions (4 px
RMS) and the land mask, flat green with a dark green coast on the PNG's #ceffff sea, the star at Naha, and all 27
labels as real `<text>` (English in Arial, black or blue italic; Japanese in MS Gothic / IPAGothic / Noto Sans CJK JP)
fitted to the PNG's lettering.

Differences: the PNG's islands carry relief shading from a raster terrain render (demis.nl), which is not reproduced
(flat fill instead), and the real coasts and islets differ from that render's generalised ones. Comparison:
`Documents\claude-screenshots\agentic-vectorization_2026-10-19\cmp_map-okinawa-pref.png`.
