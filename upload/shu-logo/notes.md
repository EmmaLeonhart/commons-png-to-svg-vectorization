Seton Hall University's text logo, built by `files/shu-logo/build.py`:
- "SETON HALL" as real `<text>` in white on the PNG's blue (#004488), over a drop shadow. The shadow is the same text
  in the PNG's darker blue (#003b76), offset (5, 5), the shift that best overlaps the PNG's shadow pixels.
- The logo's face is a Garamond-like serif with its own letter spacing. So each word is one `<text>` whose glyphs are
  placed by an `x` list, every letter fitted to its own ink columns in the PNG.
- Font stack: Garamond, EB Garamond, Adobe Garamond Pro, Times New Roman, serif.
