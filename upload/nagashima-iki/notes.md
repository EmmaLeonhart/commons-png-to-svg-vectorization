# File:Nagashima Iki.png → SVG

Built by `files/nagashima-iki/build.py` from MLIT 国土数値情報 N03 (2024) boundaries of Iki City: every island in an
equirectangular projection fitted to the PNG's land mask, Nagashima (長島, one of the three Watara islands, located by OpenStreetMap at 33.7265 N,
129.6316 E) in red, the PNG's colours and black coast, and the labels 壱岐島 and 長島 as real `<text>` fitted to the
PNG's lettering (MS Gothic, with IPAGothic / Noto Sans CJK JP as fallbacks).

The call (as for Abkhazia and Aomori): the PNG's outline is a schematic hand drawing, so the real outline replaces
it rather than tracing it; here it differs a good deal (land IoU 0.76: the real coast is far more indented, and the
PNG's long east cape and north islets are stylised). Comparison:
`Documents\claude-screenshots\agentic-vectorization_2026-10-19\cmp_nagashima-iki.png`.
