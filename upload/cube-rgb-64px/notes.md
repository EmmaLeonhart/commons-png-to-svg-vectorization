# Cube NoEdges RGBfaces 64px

- **Target:** [File:Cube NoEdges RGBfaces 64px.png](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Cube_NoEdges_RGBfaces_64px.png),
  64×64, by David Koller (2016), CC BY-SA 4.0. Three flat faces with transparent gaps.
- **Rebuilt as:** three polygons. The left and right faces are exact parallelograms (their areas equal
  their pixel counts, 896 each); the top rhombus (2,15)-(32,0)-(62,15)-(32,30) has area 900, its
  pixel count. Pure #00ff00 / #ff0000 / #0000ff as in the PNG. `shape-rendering="crispEdges"`
  keeps the hard pixel edges at 64 px. CC BY-SA 4.0 with attribution to David Koller.
- **Check:** 84 of 4096 pixels differ, all on the slanted edges (rasteriser stair-steps).
