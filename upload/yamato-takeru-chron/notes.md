# File:The Condensed Lifetime Chronologies of Yamato Takeru and Miyazuhime.png → SVG

Built by construction (`files/yamato-takeru-chron/build.py`):
- **Reign bands:** a darker label column and a lighter field for each reign, at the PNG's measured rows and colours.
- **Fades:** the white fades at the top and bottom are linear gradients, matching the measured profiles.
- **Bars:** the two lifetime bars are hexagons with the PNG's extents.
- **Labels:** every label is real `<text>`. The emperors, reign lengths and the four dated notes are fitted to the
  PNG's lettering (Meiryo Bold, with Hiragino Sans / Noto Sans CJK JP / Yu Gothic as fallbacks). 日本武尊 and 宮簀媛命
  are set vertically in the bars, one character per measured glyph box.

The emperor names come out a little heavier than in the PNG.
