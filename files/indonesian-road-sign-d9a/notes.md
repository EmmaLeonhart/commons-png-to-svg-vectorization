# Indonesian Road Sign d9a

- **Target:** [File:Indonesian Road Sign d9a.png](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Indonesian_Road_Sign_d9a.png), 888×1180,
  hospital sign per KM 61/1993, own work by AdriansyahYS. Tagged `{{SVG|road sign}}`; no SVG exists on Commons (searched).
- **Rebuilt as:** a `#000090` rounded rectangle (box and corner radius r 25.8 from the mask's area), the red cross and the
  white bed as straight-edged polygons whose corners are fitted to the colour masks (`tools/fit_polygons.py`: exact
  pixel geometry simplified with Douglas–Peucker at 1.6 px; the cross comes out as its 12 corners, the bed as 9 polygons
  with 55 corners in all). 0.92% of pixels differ; the SVG is 1.4 KB.
- **Method note:** corner-fitting is only used for shapes that are straight-edged by construction (pictograms); see INTENT.
