# File:Andechs-Wappen.png → SVG

Arms of the Counts of Andechs (Stammwappen): *Azure, in chief a lion passant and in base an eagle
displayed argent.* The PNG (LeoDavid's drawing, recoloured to silver by Dorado in 2010 as the
"Stammwappen") is a full achievement with helm and crest; this SVG is the shield.

Built from Sodacan's [File:Wappen Andechs 2.svg](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Wappen_Andechs_2.svg)
(the same arms drawn with gold charges; {{PD-Coa-Germany}}), with its gold shades shifted to argent by the
arms generator's own `recolor()` (`tools/recolor_svg.py ... or:argent`, generator 8d8ab870). The generator
cannot lay out this blazon itself (two large charges in pale come out tiny or overlapping), so Sodacan's
layout is used. Difference from the PNG: the PNG arms the beasts or (yellow claws and beak); in this SVG they
are argent like the rest of the charge. Comparison: `Documents\claude-screenshots\agentic-vectorization_2026-10-19\cmp_andechs.png`.
