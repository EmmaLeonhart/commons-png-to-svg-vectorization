# File:Tenmaya logo.png → SVG

Tenmaya's wordmark, built by construction (`files/tenmaya-logo/build.py`). Its strokes vary slightly in weight and
have round ends and rounded corners, so each letter is built from measured primitives:
- rounded rectangles with per-corner radii (half the stroke gives a round cap);
- the arches of M and A as an outer rounded shape minus an inner one;
- the N's diagonal as a parallelogram;
- the Y's bend as two half-ellipses;
- the pink stroke inside the M as a slanted polygon.

Grey #898989, pink #ee86b4. Ink IoU against the PNG is 0.966. The PNG's own edges are slightly jagged, and the
construction's are clean.

The logo was not available as vector from Tenmaya's site (it served a block page) or on Commons.
