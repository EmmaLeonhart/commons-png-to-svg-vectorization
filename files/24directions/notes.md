# 24directions (24方位)

- **Target:** [File:24directions.png](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:24directions.png), 348×350,
  by Nnh (ja.wikipedia, 2004), "MS-PAINTにて自作". GFDL (migration=relicense).
- **Rebuilt from scratch** with the construction of `files/ehou-direction/` (same author, same figure
  without the highlights and arrows): regular octagons with apothems 149 / 120.25 / 92.75 / 65.5 (identical
  to Ehou-direction, re-measured here), centre (173.75, 174.9), centre circle r 35.5, equal angular sectors
  24 / 12 / 8 / 16, 2 px lines.
- **Text:** 24 mountains, 12 branches, 8 trigrams, 中央 and 8 compass points, 53 `<text>` in total.
- **Check:** 12% of pixels differ, mostly glyph shapes and the antialiased diagonals (the original is aliased).
