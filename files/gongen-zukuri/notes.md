# Gongen Zukuri (権現造平面図)

- **Target:** [File:Gongen Zukuri.png](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Gongen_Zukuri.png), 523×642, plan of a gongen-zukuri
  shrine, own work by Hiroyuki0904 (2008).
- **Rebuilt as:** seven `#ffcc00` axis bands as rectangles (measured from the yellow components), then the aliased black line
  art as exact segments, with stacked 1 px runs merged into single lines of that width (189 line elements), and 66
  pillars as circles (`tools/rebuild_tree.extract`), drawn over the yellow as in the PNG. Every black pixel is explained;
  0.39% of pixels differ. 13 KB.
