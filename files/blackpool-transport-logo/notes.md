# Blackpool Transport simple logo

- **Target:** [File:Blackpool Transport simple logo.png](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Blackpool_Transport_simple_logo.png),
  63×131, aliased black and white, "a simple, textless version of the Blackpool Transport logo for use on
  s-rail templates", own work by Hammersfan, CC BY-SA 3.0.
- **Rebuilt as** two stroked primitives, 5 px: the tower (straight legs measured row by row, whose extensions
  meet at an apex at (32, 3.5), with splayed feet) and the base, a circular arc of r 35.3 through
  (4.5,127.5)-(31.5,~115)-(57.5,127.5). Offsets were grid-searched; IoU against the PNG is 0.88, the
  rest being aliased vs antialiased edges. CC BY-SA 3.0 with attribution.
