# Flag of the Uzbek Soviet Socialist Republic (1937-1938)

- **Target:** [File:Flag of the Uzbek Soviet Socialist Republic(1937-1938).png](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Flag_of_the_Uzbek_Soviet_Socialist_Republic(1937-1938).png),
  752×381, by Whaales (2020), drawn in paint.net from the Wikipedia description. CC BY 4.0.
- **Rebuilt from:** nothing to reuse, since the flag is a plain field with an inscription. Drawn
  as a `#cd0000` rect plus two `<text>` lines in `#ffd700`, colours taken from the PNG.
  The SVG name adds a space before the bracket. CC BY 4.0 with attribution to Whaales.
- **Method:** `python files/uzbek-ssr-flag-1937/build.py`. Font `Liberation Sans` (metric-compatible
  with Arial, available on Commons), 48 px; both lines' ink boxes match the PNG within 2 px, and
  0.58% of pixels differ.
- **Text note:** the PNG writes "Ozвekistan" with Cyrillic в, which the SVG keeps. The 1930s Uzbek
  Latin alphabet used ʙ (U+0299); whether to switch is an editorial choice for the uploader.
- **Checked:** in Chrome against the PNG.
